About

A bit about me

I'm a self-taught developer who likes building complete little worlds rather than one-off projects — which is how Forever Hub came together. It started as a single landing page and grew into an ecosystem: an AI assistant, a live weather app, a shopping experience, and this portfolio, all sharing one visual language.

I work mostly in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a growing interest in wiring real APIs into front-end projects — the kind of detail that turns a static page into something that actually does things. I care a lot about the small stuff: whether a button feels right to press, whether a layout holds up on a cracked phone screen, whether an error message actually helps instead of just existing.

Outside of code, I'm the person who'll rebuild something three times because the spacing was "almost" right. Forever Hub is very much a living project — what you're looking at right now is one part of it.
